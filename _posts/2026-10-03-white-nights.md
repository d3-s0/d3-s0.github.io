Set in the summer months in St Petersburg, Russia during a time where the where sun doesn’t set - hence white nights.
The main character is known as a dreamer who doesn’t interact with anyone. He sees a woman in the street and ends up saving her from a stranger (white knight ref). 

He starts falling in love with her the more they speak.  She talks to him and tells him story of how she is waiting 1 year for a man she is supposed to get married to but hasn’t showed up yet. Each night, they meet and chat with the main character hoping the man doesn’t show and ends up helping her contact him.

The main character is in the classic bro-zone. Eventually he expresses his love and they get together but then the guy shows up and she ends up with him. Predictable but painful ending. Ending shows him growing old still dreaming from this interaction. 

Story of why you should NOT stay in dreamer state and see things for what they are. He should have seen that she was in love with another man and moved on.
