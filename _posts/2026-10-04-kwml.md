KWML by Robert Moore. The book is about how boys should navigate life and become cognisant of 4 archetypes within their psyche: the king, warrior, magician and lover. These are based on Carl Jung’s collective unconscious- instincts patterns and energy config inherited in species.  The initial problem stated is that there is a lack of rituals for boys to become men so a lot of modern world is full of boys instead of men.

The formula for a correct process of boys to men is: death of boy ego (reliance on mother) +  ritual leader (guides boy) + sacred space (faces trials)

King - Tyrant vs Weakling
Boy Warrior - Bully vs Coward  -> Warrior: Sadist vs Masochist
Boy Magician - Trickster vs The dummy -> Magician: Manipulator vs Denying “innocent” one
Boy Lover -  Mama’s boy vs dreamer - > Lover: Addicted lover vs Impotent lover
