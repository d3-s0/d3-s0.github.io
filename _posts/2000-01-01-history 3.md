#### medieval europe

Began when roman empire collapsed, Charlemagne, king of france took over + crowned roman emperror. powerful christian ruler. vikings attacked! feudal system kings, nobles, vassals, knights, serfs. guilds were formed to set prices and keep standard of quality. crusades weakened power of nobles. 1100s, Charlemagne holy roman empire idea of nation building. spain, france, england. norman conquest, william of normandy took over england 1066. 1200, William's successor John taxed the people which led to the Magna Carta (Charter that limited king's power). King had to consult a law making council called the parliament. hundred year war with france. the plague 1347BLACK DEATH. In 610, rise of islam prophet muhammad heard message from god from the quran. 5 pillars of islam: testimony of faith, 5 times a day, give to the needy, fast for ramadan, make a pilgrimage to mecca. fast during ramadan, hajj to city of mecca

Crusades were military expeditions to control of holy land of Jerusalem from Muslims. Pope Urban II called for Christians to free Jerusalem. Pope wanted the power and stop Muslims in the Jerusalem. First Crusades, 1096, lost a lot of people getting there. French reached and massacred muslims in 1099. Further crusades went back and forth 9 in total. The 1204 Sack of Constantinople: During the Fourth Crusade, Western European crusaders attacked, pillaged, and occupied the capital of the Byzantine Empire. Crusades made split between eastern and western christainty more permament.
