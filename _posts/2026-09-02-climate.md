Climate change is the long-term change in Earth’s weather patterns e.g. Temp, Rain, Wind primarily driven by human activities. 

### How did the temperature go up?
Earth's climate is made up of layers which allows us to flourish.
Incoming solar radiation from the Sun, high energy passes through the clouds and earth warms up. Greenhouses gasses absorb more energy causing warm gasses that heat surface like a greenhouse. Less heat escaping means our stable climate that let us flourish for the past 10,000 years are numbered.  

The activities include:
- Human’s burning fossil fuels: increased co2 (largest driver - approx 75%)
- Human’s deforestation: stops co2 sinks
- Human’s agricultural use: stops co2 sink and increased methane

### What will happen?
- tropical diseases
- farm crops
- sea level rises
- droughts
- infrastructure breaks
- food supply
- burning rainforests
- acid oceans
- mass extinction

### How do we fix it?
The main ways:
- get off fossil fuels
- stop deforestation
- reduce agricultural use

On an individual level, your carbon footprint can be reduced by: 
- Eating less meat
- Turn thermostat down
- Take public transport
- Buy local 
- Switch off electrical equipment when not in use
- Reuse and recycle
