Caring for earth. We need to make sure it doesn’t go to shit. Earth is reaching the point of saturation. We have an edge for development and must look to plateau our development.  Growth cannot always be infinite.
We must strive to fix it. But first we must understand the human condition and the tools humanity have constructed. We can then use the tools to make the world a better place. What is Climate change? Climate change is the long-term change in Earth’s weather patterns e.g. Temp, Rain, Wind primarily driven by human activities. Human activities have led to more heat trapping gases being released which is increasing global temperatures.
What causes climate change?
- Human’s burning fossil fuels
- Human’s deforestation
- Human’s agricultural use
- Indicators

How do we fix it?
First, we must measure what we need to fix. Model it. The new model to understand development is the doughnut model. 
We can’t completely fix it but we can limit impact by reaching net zero. The Net zero is when amount of greenhouse gases released is equal to amount of greenhouse gas removed. Decarbonisation across sectors e.g. energy, transport and agriculture
