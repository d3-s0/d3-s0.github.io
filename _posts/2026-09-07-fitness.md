I wanted a way to measure my overall strength. That's where I thought a single metric to indicate my strength level at any time would be most useful. It needs to be based on exercises that can be measured and not subjective. The key exercises I landed on were:
- bench press
- overhead press
- pull ups
- squat

These exercises cover the vast majority of muscles in the human body. 

Data sources for reference points:
- Bench press: https://strengthlevel.com/strength-standards/bench-press
- Overhead press: https://strengthlevel.com/strength-standards/shoulder-press
- Pull ups: https://strengthlevel.com/strength-standards/pull-ups
- Squat: https://strengthlevel.com/strength-standards/squat


The current aim is to get to intermediate stage for my age band.
