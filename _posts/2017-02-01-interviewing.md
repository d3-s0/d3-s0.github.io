Interviews are tough. They are a nerve racking experience. Ironically you perform the best when you are your most relaxed. 
However, do not fret - there is a skill to interviewing. 
The best way to interview is to know all the projects you have completed and structure them in the STAR framework when answering.

- **Situation**: Give context to the problem
- **Task**: What was YOUR responsibility?
- **Action**: What did YOU do exactly?
- **Result**: What was the measurable result? Feedback or lesson learnt?

I recommend preparing answers to common interview questions since at the fundamental level they all ask the same thing.

- Who are you as a person?
- Are the projects you have worked on relevant to the company you are applying for?

**Confidence**
Confidence is self-awareness and acceptance.
Firstly, accept who you are currently.
Tell yourself why YOU are right for the role.
What have you accomplished already? Have a list of accomplishments.

The vacancy is due to someone having a problem. You hopefully, can help.

These questions are from the book why you? by James Reed.
