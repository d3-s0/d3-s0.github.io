### first civilisations
First civilisation grew up independently in Mesopotamia, Egypt, Indus Valley, China, Mesoamerica, and the Andes.

#### mesopotamia
<img src="/imgs/history/mesotomia.PNG" alt="Universe" style="width: 100%; height: auto; display: block">
Sumeria was the first civilisation in mesopotamia. City states with trade happening via religious temples. Made concept of time we use now 60 sec = 1 min. Babylon lived in western half.Assyria empire came about 1400BCE.Medes, persia and scythians defeated assyria in 612 BCE.Babylons and chaldeans rose again + identified more planets.

#### ancient africa
<img src="/imgs/history/ancient_africa.PNG" alt="Universe" style="width: 100%; height: auto; display: block">

Egypt had 3 phases: old, middle, new kingdom. Pyramids were built. Great spinx was built to protect the pyramid. King tutankhamen or king tut. Alexander the great took egypt. After cleopatra dies romans take over.

#### ancient india
<img src="/imgs/history/ancient_india.PNG" alt="Universe" style="width: 100%; height: auto; display: block">

India is separated from other countries by himalayas. People managed to get through and settled in indus valley. The indus river flows from the himalayas and down to arabian sea in the north west. On the north east the ganges river comes from himalayas. The two big rivers give fertile land for farming. Around 1500BCE, aryans moved in from central asia and mixed with indus valley people. India had a social caste system called varnas.  Hinduism came about then too. Hindus believe there is one spirtual power and is a polytheistic relgion. The beliefs are found in the upanishads (sacred text) reincarnation and karma. If you lead a good life, you will be freed from cycle of death. You must follow your dharma (duty). Around 300BCE, the maurya empire ruled. Then the gupta empire ruled. 

#### ancient china
<img src="/imgs/history/silk_road.png" alt="Universe" style="width: 100%; height: auto; display: block">

Shang dynasty. Zhou dynasty. First emperor of china (qin ruler) great wall of china. Han dynasty. Trade began through silk road (china to mediterranean sea).China exported silk (from silk worms), spices, tea, jade. Silk road was more of a network of routes. Buddhism was introduced to china from indian merchants. Family came first in the chinese culture. duty to respect the head of family. filial piety. Confucianism (551BCE): believed there are 4 human relationships: ruler and ruled, friend and friend, husband and wife, older and younger sibling, father and son. Taoism: happiness comes from from a balanced life: nature, simple living and selfless lives.


#### ancient greece 
<img src="/imgs/history/ancient_greece.PNG" alt="Migration" style="width: 100%; height: auto; display: block">
<img src="/imgs/history/greece.webp" alt="Universe" style="width: 100%; height: auto; display: block">
Minoans lived in crete. mycenaeans took over 1400BCE. 1194BCE Trojan war decline of greeks and start of the dark ages in greece. Homer writes about Trojan war in his epic poem Iliad. 800BCE city states began to form new type of ruling called democracy was made! 700BCE athens was popular! Greek gods were a big part of the culture. olympic games made: running, jumping, discus, wresting, boxing. athens and sparta were the two big city states. athens wrote plays and made pottery, sold goods and philosophy, 100k slaves. 
Greek philosophy. socrates: socratic method  asking questions. executed by made to drink poison. plato: studied under socrates. believed society should be split into: workers, soldiers, and philosopher-rulers. Taught a code of ethics. Aristotle: studied under plato. defined categories of logic, biology and physics. found his own school lyceum. He tutored alexander the great!
Greek art: they wrote tragedies and comedies with dialogue mixed with chorus singing. aeschylus, sophocles and euripides were main playwrights. Homer's Iliad and Odyssey came about. Architecture: parthenon for athens. Sparta focused on battle. they had slaves who farmed the land. Athens and sparta started fighting in the poloponnesian wars. Sparta won. Kingdom of Macedonia (north of greece). He conquered Greek city states through bribes and threats but was then assassinated. He took Aristotle to teach his son culture. Alexander the great took over at age 20 taking Persia, Egypt, parts of India

#### ancient rome
<img src="/imgs/history/ancient_rome.PNG" alt="Migration" style="width: 100%; height: auto; display: block">
Roman Kingdom. Roman Republic. Roman Empire.
Etrusans ruled as tyrants, eventually romans took over and formed the Roman republic. In a republic citizen with right to vote elect leader + had a senate to pass laws. The classes were: patricians: wealthy upper class men in the senate/ plebeians: ordinary citizens. consuls: two officials who enforced laws. praetors: judges. romans took: Carthage, Greece, Spain, Gaul(france). Julias Caesar: military commander shared rule with 2 others. Eventually he took over. He was eventually killed by Senators. Second Triumvirate formed: Mark Antony, Octavian and Lepidus. Octavian eventually took over and was given title 'Augustus' the first emperor. Colosseum was built: amphitheater where gladiators fought. Forums: public city centers where people could trade, visit temples, vote for leaders and meet friends. Romans built roads, aqueducts, lots of baths. Roman laws: innocent until proven guilty, more kids mean more power for women. Rise of Christianity. Romans took a lot of greek gods but renamed them. Romans were generally open to most religions but then Christianity began to stand out. Christianity: based on jewish man named Jesus. Jesus said there was only one god. Roman emperor thought Jesus might revolt against government he condemned him to death. According to the gospels, Jesus returned to life and told his disciples. Paul his first follower, wrote letters and traveled to spread the word. Christianity grew and Romans made it a crime to be Christian. Emperor constantine. Helped spread Christianity. First Christian Roman emperor. Moved capital of Rome to Byzantium (Turkey) -> Constantinople -> Istanbul. Decline. Emperor Commodus: did not care for senate, poor choices led to fall. German invaders took over the west empire.  Constantinople held on as the Byzantine empire. justinian emperor sorted roman laws to make the justinian code. slowly the empire fell. two forms of christianity formed: roman catholic church, eastern orthodox church. byzantine grew again but then crusades and threats from ottoman empire won. 1453 turks took over.
