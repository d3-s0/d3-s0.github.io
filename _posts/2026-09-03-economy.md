Most the modern world works off a mixed economy: free + government rules.
Free market:
- Supply & Demand: Buyers and sellers set prices
- Private Property: Individuals own resources and businesses.
- Profit Motive: Financial gain drives production and innovation.
- Voluntary Exchange: Trades happen only when both sides benefit

Government intervenes:
- Taxes, laws, infrastructure, police, and defense
- Regulations prevent monopolies and fix environmental harm

## UK economy 
The UK economy is services (80%), manufacturing & construction (15%) and agriculture & energy (5%). Economic policy can be split into:
- **Monetary policy**: Bank of England maintain price stability by targeting an inflation rate of 2%, primarily achieved by adjusting the benchmark interest rate
- **Fiscal policy**: Fiscal policy is the use of government spending and taxation to affect country's economy. Income via taxes fund public spending: NHS, welfare, education, defence and infrastructure.
- **Regulation**: government steps in to break monopolies, protect consumers and ensure financial stability.
