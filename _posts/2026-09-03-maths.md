# Geometry	
Euclidean (flat surface)
    Basics: point, line and plane
    Angles and Lines (1D)
    Polygons (2D) – triangles, quadrilaterals, perimeter and area
    Circles
    3D shapes – cubes, spheres, cones

Curved
    Spheres, bends, warps
    Stretching or bending without tearing or gluing perspective
 
# Algebra
Replace numbers with letters that’s algebra! It lets us write expressions and find universal rules that work for every number, not just one specific case. 
Polynomial equations (expressions raised to whole number powers)
    Linear equations
    Quadratic equations
    Cubic equations
Linear algebra
    Vectors: Ordered lists of numbers that represent both magnitude and direction. 
    Matrices: Rectangular arrays of numbers used to store data or represent linear transformations. The transformation takes a vector and makes it into another vector.

 
# Calculus
Differential Calculus
    Differential: rate of change: dy/dx Studies rates of change and slopes of curves through limits, derivatives, and applications like optimization and motion analysis. 
    Models dynamic systems using derivatives, solving ordinary (ODEs) and partial (PDEs) equations for real-world phenomena.
Integral Calculus
    Integral: accumulation of quantities ∫_a^b▒x^2  dx
