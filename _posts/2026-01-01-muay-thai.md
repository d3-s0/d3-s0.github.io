Muay thai is a striking martial art. It is often referred to as the art of 8 limbs because it uses both arms, elbows, knees, and feet to strike. I signed up to Muay thai in May 2022. I had put on quite a lot of weight during COVID and i wanted to shed it. Since then, I have been going about once a week on average at least. I very recently signed up to twice a week. I love Muay Thai, for several reasons:
- Few sports exercise as many parts of the body
- The community aspect, everyone is really nice. Enhances the tribal feeling
- The cardio benefits, each session burns about 1000 calories
- It's self-defence - I feel more physically confident
- Post workout endorphins - after the workout + shower

After my first class, I couldn't move for ages. Body was aching and I struggled to remember long combinations. I was constantly thinking "oh I am going to look stupid I can't remember simple moves". However, the body is an amazing thing, it can adapt remarkably over time. Now I find it much easier to remember the moves.
