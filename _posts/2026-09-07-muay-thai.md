## Introduction
Muay thai is a striking martial art. It is often referred to as the art of 8 limbs because it uses both arms, elbows, knees, and feet to strike. I signed up to Muay thai in May 2022. I had put on quite a lot of weight during COVID and i wanted to shed it. Since then, I have been going about once a week on average at least. I very recently signed up to twice a week. I love Muay Thai, for several reasons:
•	Few sports exercise as many parts of the body
•	The community aspect, everyone is really nice. Enhances the tribal feeling
•	The cardio benefits, each session burns about 1000 calories
•	It's self defence - I feel more physically confident
•	Post workout endorphins - after the workout + shower

After my first class, I couldn't move for ages. Body was aching and I struggled to remember long combinations. I was constantly thinking "oh I am going to look stupid I can't remember simple moves". However, the body is an amazing thing, it can adapt remarkably over time. Now I find it much easier to remember the moves. Eventually, I want to go to Thailand to experience the muay thai culture.

 The stance
The foundation of Muay Thai starts with proper stance and footwork. A strong stance provides balance and power, while effective footwork allows for fluid movement and positioning. 

## Punching 
Jab
-	Lead hand 
-	Hold elbow low as long as possible
-	Other hand protects face
-	When you jab turn hip and shift weight to front foot for power
-	Use jab to disturb timing 

Cross
-	Straight line 
-	Turn and shift weight to front foot
-	Make sure punch is explosive
Upper cut
-	Lower hand and hit from below
-	Quickly return to position

## Kicking
Round house kick
-	Line up with center line to target
-	Entry of kick:
-	45-degree step and out
-	open ankle towards the bag
-	bring rear arm and touch lead shoulder + lead arm comes to hip
-	Kick movement
-	Swing rear arm and pull your hip up to the target
-	Whole of body should be past the center line
-	Knee comes up vertically and just before impact it cuts/turns into the target but arcing at the very end - lead with the knee and go across the target.
-	Your hip only snaps a second before impact
-	At point of impact knee + hip should be past center line
-	imagine cutting through the target - like a baseball bat going through
-	Don't swing out the leg - stay in
-	Arm swing - harder you swing that arm the more force you will drive through
-	breathe out - yell to get the energy out
-	Set the intention -tell yourself it's going to be faster.
-	Returning from the kick
-	Exit from the kick fast - you don't want to get whacked/foot grabbed

Switch kick 
-	Stay level during the switch to avoid telegraphing your intent - Use the switch kick to set up other techniques, like a double switch followed by punches.
