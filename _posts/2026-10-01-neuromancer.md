Neuromancer by William Gibson. The book is a tough read if you don’t read often. The writer is clearly aware of how mature sci-fi is at the time so doesn’t bother to explain a lot of info about the world. Instead choosing to drop the reader in the world and make the reader form the world in their minds. It was a slog for me. It took me months to read bits here and there, but I wasn't reading a lot then so that might have played a part.

Case a young computer hacker who stole from his employer was punished by his CNS
being damaged. He makes his way to Chiba, Japan where he hopes to fix his body but
finds himself swallowed whole by the underworld.

Case gets recruited by Wintermute (an AI) via Corto to go on a heist along with Molly (the muscle - ultimate cybernetic samurai - retractable blades, reflexes), Riveria (illusion guy), Dixie (his dead mentor). The big heist is all so Wintermute can merge with another AI called Neuromancer. Wintermute is the rational AI with no personality who manipulates everyone ultimately but cannot predict random human behaviour.

It is revealed the major corps made the two AIs to have more power but Winter mute ends up hating them. Neuromancer is the other AI the major corp made who has a personality. Neuromancer tries to bait Case into staying in cyberspace with fake Linda but Case ultimately rejects the offer which spells the end for Neuromancer.

The title neuromancer combines neuro for brain and necromancer who talks to the dead to represent possibility to talk to the dead using AI.

Ultimately, Case destroys the major corp with a virus and Wintermute and Neuromancer merge successfully. The new AI seems disinterested in humans and instead chooses to talk to other AI.
Case and Molly live together a while but Molly ends up leaving.

All the characters suffer from some sort of addiction and/or trauma - Molly over her ex lover, Case over drugs and addiction to digital escape, rivera to drugs and Corto has PTSD from war.

The book was written in 1984 but very relevant to current times with possibility of AI takeover going around the media.
The ultimate meaning I think is a bit bleak though, it says how to survive like Case and Molly in the brave new world you have to merge with tech but at the same time if you lose your humanity you become easily manipulated by AI since you are too predictable since you have lost the human spirit.
